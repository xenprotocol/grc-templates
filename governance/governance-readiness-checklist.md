> **Purpose:** Assesses whether governance is mature enough to formalize — before the ISSC is chartered and before policies are signed. Each unchecked item is a gap to close first.
>
> **How to use this template:** Complete honestly with the CEO or executive sponsor. Assign one owner per gap, record gaps in the risk register, and re-run the checklist before formal launch and annually thereafter.

---

## 1. LEADERSHIP

- [ ] CEO sponsorship is explicit and communicated to the organization
- [ ] Management commitment statement drafted
- [ ] Security reporting line to senior management defined
- [ ] Risk appetite statement approved

## 2. STRUCTURE

- [ ] ISSC established
- [ ] ISSC charter signed by the CEO
- [ ] Membership defined by role (not individual), with voting rights
- [ ] Meeting cadence and quorum rules agreed
- [ ] Minute-taking and document control process in place

## 3. POLICIES

- [ ] Policy architecture defined (Level 1 top-level, Level 2 domain, Level 3 procedural)
- [ ] Information Security Policy (Level 1) drafted
- [ ] Required Level 2 policies identified and owners assigned
- [ ] Document control (version, owner, review cycle) applied to every policy
- [ ] Policy communication and acknowledgement process defined

## 4. RISK

- [ ] Risk register exists and is populated with initial risks
- [ ] Risk Assessment Methodology (RAM) adopted
- [ ] Risk owners assigned to all material risks
- [ ] Risk treatment plan in place with dates

## 5. ROLES, REPORTING, AND METRICS

- [ ] RACI defined for key processes (risk assessment, incident response, access management)
- [ ] Reporting cadence agreed (ISSC monthly, executive quarterly)
- [ ] Metrics defined (KPIs and KRIs, e.g., % access reviews completed, time to patch)
- [ ] Baseline metric values collected
