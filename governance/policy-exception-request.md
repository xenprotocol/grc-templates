> **Purpose:** Formalizes a temporary, documented deviation from a policy or control. An undocumented exception is a finding waiting to happen; a documented, compensated, time-boxed exception is auditable governance.
>
> **How to use this template:** One form per exception. Complete Sections 1–4; score the risk with the Risk Assessment Methodology (RAM). Route for approval per Section 7 — the level depends on the risk score. File the approved form with the policy it excepts and track expiry in the exception register.
