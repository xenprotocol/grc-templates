> **Purpose:** Defines how internal audits are planned, executed, reported,
> and followed up. ISO 27001 Clause 9.2 requires audits at planned
> intervals against the ISMS requirements and the SoA. This procedure makes
> the audit program repeatable and defensible.

---

## 1. AUDIT PLANNING

1.1. An **annual audit plan** is prepared covering:

- All ISO 27001 main body Clauses (4-10)
- A sample of Annex A controls from each theme (A.5.x, A.6.x, A.7.x, A.8.x)
- High-risk processes and recent changes (risk-prioritized)

1.2. Audit frequency is risk-based: critical processes audited at least
annually; lower-risk areas on a rolling two-year cycle.

1.3. Auditors must be independent of the area audited (Clause 9.2.5).
Internal auditors do not audit their own work.

## 2. AUDIT EXECUTION

2.1. Each audit follows the steps:

| Step | Activity | Evidence |
| :---- | :------- | :------- |
| 1 | Opening meeting: scope, criteria, logistics | Meeting record |
| 2 | Document review: policies, SoA, records | Reviewed documents |
| 3 | Fieldwork: interviews, observation, sampling | Working papers |
| 4 | Testing: control verification (config checks, log review, live verification) | Test results |
| 5 | Closing meeting: preliminary findings | Meeting record |
| 6 | Report issuance | Audit Report |

2.2. **Sampling** — a representative sample of records is tested (e.g., 10
access reviews, 5 terminations, 3 change tickets). Sample sizes are
documented in the working papers.

2.3. **Finding classification:**

| Classification | Definition | Example |
| :------------- | :----------- | :------- |
| **Major NC** | Significant failure; system does not meet requirements | No risk assessment performed |
| **Minor NC** | Isolated failure; system largely effective | One stale access review |
| **Observation** | Risk of future nonconformity; improvement opportunity | Missing evidence of training |
| **Good Practice** | Commendable practice exceeding requirements | Automated evidence collection |

## 3. REPORTING

3.1. The audit report contains:

- Scope, criteria, and auditor
- Summary of coverage
- Findings with: requirement cited (Clause or A.X.XX), evidence, impact,
  recommendation
- Auditee responses
- Overall conclusion and audit opinion

3.2. Reports are issued within [10] working days of the closing meeting
and distributed to the auditee, the CISO, and management.

## 4. CORRECTIVE ACTION AND FOLLOW-UP

4.1. Nonconformities require a **Corrective Action Plan** (see template):
root cause analysis (5-Whys), correction (immediate fix), corrective
action (prevent recurrence), owner, and target date.

4.2. Follow-up verification is performed at the agreed date. Findings
remain open until verified closed.

4.3. Audit results are an input to the management review (Clause 9.3.2(c)).

## 5. RECORDS

5.1. Audit records (plans, working papers, reports, corrective actions)
are retained for [3] years in accordance with the Retention Policy.
