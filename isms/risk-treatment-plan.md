> **Purpose:** Documents the treatment decision for every risk marked
> tolerable or intolerable: what will be done, who owns it, by when, and
> what the target residual score is. This is the commitment the internal
> audit tests against (Clause 6.1.3).

> **How to use this template:** one treatment plan entry per risk. Reference
> the Risk Register risk ID. Map each control to its Annex A number — this
> is how the Statement of Applicability is born.
