> **Purpose:** Formal, documented acceptance of residual risk. This is the
> artifact that turns "we accepted it" from a placeholder into a defensible
> decision. ISO 27001 Clause 6.1.3 requires residual risk to be evaluated
> and documented — this form is that documentation.

> **How to use this template:** complete ONE form per accepted risk.
> Without a completed form, a risk is NOT accepted — it is deferred.
