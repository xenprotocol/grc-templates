## REGISTER RULES

1. **Score, not count** — the top 10 scores consume 80% of attention.
2. **Verdicts** follow the Risk Assessment Methodology: 1-4 Acceptable,
   5-9 Tolerable, 10-15 Intolerable, 16-25 Critical.
3. **Residual score** is re-evaluated after treatment — never assumed.
4. A risk closes only when: residual risk is within appetite, the owner
   signs the Risk Acceptance Form, and evidence of reduction is documented.

## STATUS DEFINITIONS

| Status | Definition |
| :------ | :----------- |
| **Open** | Identified, evaluation pending |
| **Treatment** | Treatment plan in progress |
| **Accepted** | Residual risk formally accepted by owner |
| **Transferred** | Risk shifted to third party (insurance/cloud) |
| **Closed** | Residual risk within appetite, evidence documented |
