> **Purpose:** Evaluates the security posture of suppliers before
> contracting and periodically thereafter. Implements the supplier
> security controls (A.5.19-A.5.23) and supports DORA third-party risk
> management where applicable.

> **How to use this template:** complete ONE assessment per supplier.
> Score each domain 1-5 (1 = no controls, 5 = verified strong). Total
> score feeds the vendor risk tier and the contract terms.

## 3. VENDOR RISK TIER

| Total Score | Tier | Contract Requirements |
| :---------- | :--- | :------------------- |
| 32-40 | Low Risk | Standard terms, annual review |
| 24-31 | Medium Risk | Enhanced terms, quarterly review, security addendum |
| 16-23 | High Risk | Mandatory remediation plan, right-to-audit, exit plan |
| < 16 | Critical Risk | Do not contract without CISO approval + compensating controls |
