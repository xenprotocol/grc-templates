> **Purpose:** Records which ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls apply to
> the ISMS and why. Every excluded control needs a justification grounded
> in the risk assessment (Clause 6.1.3(d)) — generic "not applicable"
> statements fail certification audits.

> **How to use this template:** complete one row per Annex A control
> (93 total). "Applied" = control implemented (reference the implementing
> document). "Not Applied" = justified exclusion referencing the risk
> assessment result. Keep this document current — it is the first thing
> the auditor asks for.

## 3. SOA UPDATE TRIGGERS

The SoA is reviewed and updated when (Clause 6.1.3(d)):

1. New risks are identified
2. Scope changes
3. Controls are implemented
4. Regulations change
5. Risk treatment outcomes change (control replaced/downgraded/compensating)
6. Risk assessment methodology or criteria are revised
7. Annual review
