## 1. PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVES

1.1. This policy establishes the framework for managing information security
across [ORG NAME] (hereafter "the Organization").

1.2. The objectives of the ISMS are to:

- Protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets
- Meet applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations
- Manage information security risk to a level acceptable to senior management
- Continually improve the effectiveness of the ISMS (Clause 10.1)

## 3. AUTHORITY AND RESPONSIBILITY

3.1. The **Chief Executive Officer** owns this policy and provides final
authority for the ISMS.

3.2. The **CISO / Information Security Manager** is responsible for
implementing, maintaining, and continually improving the ISMS, and reports
to senior management at least annually (Clause 9.3).

3.3. The **Information Security Steering Committee (ISSC)** oversees the
ISMS, reviews risk treatment progress, and approves major changes.
See the ISSC Charter template.

3.4. **All employees and contractors** are responsible for complying with
this policy and the subordinate policies, and for reporting security
incidents and weaknesses without delay.

## 4. RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK

4.1. Information security risk is managed in accordance with the Risk
Assessment Methodology (RAM). Risk assessment is performed at least
annually and whenever significant changes occur (Clause 6.1).

4.2. Risk treatment follows the four options defined in the RAM: mitigate,
transfer, avoid, or accept. Residual risk is documented in the Risk
Register and accepted by the risk owner.

4.3. The Statement of Applicability (SoA) documents which Annex A controls
apply and the justification for exclusions, in accordance with Clause
6.1.3(d).

## 5. CONTROL FRAMEWORK

5.1. The Organization applies the controls of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A as
determined by risk treatment. Controls are organized under the four themes:

| Theme | Control Range | Count |
| :------ | :----------- | :----- |
| Organizational | A.5.1–A.5.37 | 37 |
| People | A.6.1–A.6.8 | 8 |
| Physical | A.7.1–A.7.14 | 14 |
| Technological | A.8.1–A.8.34 | 34 |

5.2. Subordinate policies and procedures implement these controls. See the
Policy Architecture appendix in the book for the full document tree.

## 6. COMPLIANCE

6.1. The Organization complies with all applicable legal and regulatory
requirements, including but not limited to: GDPR, NIS2, DORA (where
applicable), the EU AI Act, and the Cyber Resilience Act (where applicable).

6.2. Compliance is verified through the internal audit program (Clause 9.2)
and management review (Clause 9.3).

## 7. REPORTING AND REVIEW

7.1. This policy is reviewed at planned intervals (at least annually) or
when significant changes occur (Clause 7.5).

7.2. Proposed changes are submitted to the ISSC for approval and to the
CEO for sign-off.
