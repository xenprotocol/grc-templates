> **Purpose:** Phased 90-day plan for establishing information security and
> GRC governance in a new organization, new role, or new program. Worked
> through phase by phase; each unchecked box is a risk to governance
> maturity.

---

## 1. PHASE 1 — ASSESS (WEEKS 1-2)

- [ ] Map stakeholders and existing governance bodies (board, executive, risk)
- [ ] Inventory applicable obligations (GDPR, NIS2, DORA, EU AI Act, CRA, sector rules)
- [ ] Assess current state: policies, risk posture, controls, resources
- [ ] Identify quick wins and critical gaps

## 2. PHASE 2 — CHARTER (WEEKS 3-4)

- [ ] Draft governance charter: mandate, membership, cadence, authority
- [ ] Establish steering committee and meeting schedule
- [ ] Define RACI for security and risk decisions
- [ ] Agree risk appetite and reporting lines under the Three Lines Model (IIA 2020)

## 3. PHASE 3 — POLICIES (WEEKS 5-8)

- [ ] Draft top-level information security policy
- [ ] Prioritize subordinate policies from the risk assessment
- [ ] Route policies through legal, HR, and relevant review bodies
- [ ] Obtain formal approval and publish with document control

## 4. PHASE 4 — OPERATE (WEEKS 9-12)

- [ ] Stand up the risk register and first formal risk assessment
- [ ] Launch management review cadence (ISO 27001 Clause 9.3)
- [ ] Begin security awareness training
- [ ] Report first governance dashboard to the steering committee

> **How to use this template:** Work with the sponsor weekly. Escalate any
> phase not completed on time at the day-30 and day-60 checkpoints. After
> day 90, transition the plan into the normal governance calendar.
