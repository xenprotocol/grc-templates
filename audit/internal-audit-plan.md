> **Purpose:** The annual program of internal audits: what will be
> audited, when, by whom, and against which criteria. ISO 27001 Clause
> 9.2 requires audits at planned intervals covering the ISMS
> requirements and the SoA. The plan is risk-based — critical processes
> audited more frequently.

> **How to use this template:** list every audit engagement for the
> year. Ensure full coverage of Clauses 4-10 across the program and a
> representative sample of Annex A controls from each theme.
