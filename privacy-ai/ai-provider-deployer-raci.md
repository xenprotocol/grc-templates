> **Purpose:** This matrix assigns responsibility for the EU AI Act obligations that apply to the Organization as AI provider, deployer, or both. It closes the gaps that arise when teams assume "someone else" owns a compliance obligation.

> **How to use this template:** R = Responsible (does the work), A = Accountable (answers for it), C = Consulted (input required), I = Informed (kept in the loop). Every obligation has exactly one A. Tailor rows to your role — a pure deployer has no provider obligations.

## 2. ROLE DEFINITIONS

2.1. **AI Provider:** develops an AI system or has it developed and places it on the market or puts it into service (Art. 3(3)).

2.2. **AI Deployer:** uses an AI system in the course of professional activity (Art. 3(4)).

2.3. **Data Owner:** accountable for data quality, provenance, and lawful basis of the data the system uses.

2.4. **CISO / GRC:** operates governance, risk, and assurance activities; escalates to the AI Governance Board.

2.5. **Legal:** interprets obligations and interfaces with supervisory authorities.

## 3. GOVERNANCE NOTES

3.1. Where the Organization is both provider and deployer, provider rows carry the higher duty and deployer rows remain in force.

3.2. Third-party providers are contractually obligated to meet their rows (Art. 25); contract reviews confirm alignment. Obligations apply on the EU AI Act timetable: prohibitions Feb 2, 2025; GPAI Aug 2, 2025; Annex III high-risk Aug 2, 2026; Annex I Aug 2, 2027.
