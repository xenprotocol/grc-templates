> **Purpose:** The ROPA is the organization's inventory of personal data processing, required by Article 30 of the GDPR (in force 2018). It demonstrates accountability, supports data subject rights, and is the first document a supervisory authority requests during an investigation or audit.

> **How to use this template:** Complete one row per processing activity in Section 2. Keep the register current — Article 30 requires it to be available to the supervisory authority on request. Never delete historical rows; mark them superseded.

## 4. REVIEW AND MAINTENANCE

4.1. This ROPA is reviewed annually and updated whenever a new processing activity starts, an existing one changes materially, or a processor is added or replaced.

4.2. Under the Three Lines Model (IIA 2020): first-line teams notify the DPO of processing changes; the DPO (second line) maintains and quality-assures the register; internal audit (third line) samples its accuracy.
