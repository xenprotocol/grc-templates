> **Purpose:** A DPIA documents the risks to data subjects of a processing operation and how they are mitigated, as required by Article 35 GDPR (in force 2018). DPIAs are mandatory where processing is likely to result in a high risk to individuals — including systematic monitoring, large-scale special category data, and new technologies such as AI systems.

> **How to use this template:** Complete the trigger assessment in Section 1 first. If any trigger is met, complete the full DPIA before processing starts. Where residual high risk remains after mitigation, consult the supervisory authority under Article 36 prior to processing.
