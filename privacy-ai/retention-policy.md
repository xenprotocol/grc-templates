> **Purpose:** This policy defines how long each category of organizational data is retained and how it is deleted or destroyed at the end of its period, supporting the GDPR Article 5(1)(e) storage limitation principle (in force 2018) and the Organization's legal, regulatory, and business obligations.

> **How to use this template:** Map every data store in scope to a row in the schedule. Where law and business need conflict, the longer period applies and is justified in the schedule. Apply a legal hold before any deletion when litigation or investigation is pending.

---

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1. This policy applies to all personal and business data held by the Organization, in all media: electronic, paper, email, backups, and cloud services.

1.2. Retention periods are set by legal obligation, regulatory requirement, or documented business need — never by default.

## 3. REVIEW CADENCE

3.1. The schedule is reviewed annually and when legal requirements change (e.g., new obligations under NIS2, DORA, or the EU AI Act become applicable).

3.2. Data owners confirm their rows at each review; the DPO quality-assures the review under the Three Lines Model (IIA 2020).
