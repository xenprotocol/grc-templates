> **Purpose:** This policy defines how employees, contractors, and third parties may use AI systems — procured, provided, or free-to-use — in the course of work. It controls shadow AI, protects personal and confidential data, and ensures high-risk uses are approved before deployment.

> **How to use this template:** Distribute with the acknowledgment in Section 6. Re-issue whenever the approved provider list changes or a new AI capability is introduced.

---

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1. This policy applies to all users of AI systems, including generative AI assistants, embedded AI features, and custom-built models.

1.2. "AI system" means software that generates outputs such as content, predictions, recommendations, or decisions influencing outcomes, per the EU AI Act definition.

## 2. PERMITTED USES

2.1. Users may use approved AI systems for:

- Drafting and summarizing documents and correspondence
- Code generation and review assistance [within approved tools: ______]
- Data analysis and insight generation on non-sensitive data
- [Other approved uses: ______]

2.2. Outputs are unverified drafts: users remain responsible for the accuracy, completeness, and compliance of any output they use or publish.

## 3. PROHIBITED USES

3.1. Users must not:

- Use unapproved ("shadow") AI systems for work purposes (Control A.8.9)
- Input personal data, special category data, or confidential information into unapproved systems
- Use AI for high-risk purposes (e.g., recruitment decisions, credit decisions, biometric identification) without prior approval from the AI Governance Board
- Create deceptive content presented as authentic (deepfakes) without clear labeling and authorization
- Rely on AI outputs where human review is required by law or policy

## 4. PROVIDER VETTING AND DATA HANDLING

4.1. New AI providers are vetted by GRC and Procurement before approval, covering: data residency, sub-processors, retention of prompts, model training on submitted data, and contractual data protection terms.

4.2. The approved provider list is maintained at: [location: ______]

4.3. Users must apply the least-sensitive data necessary and must not paste personal data into AI tools unless the tool is approved for that data class.

## 5. INCIDENT REPORTING

5.1. Suspected data exposure, unauthorized use, or AI-related incidents are reported to the Security Team without delay: [contact: ______]

5.2. Known or suspected breaches of this policy are investigated and may result in disciplinary action.
