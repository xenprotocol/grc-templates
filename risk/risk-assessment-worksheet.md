> **Purpose:** The working document for a single risk assessment —
> capturing the asset, threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood/impact
> scoring, and the treatment recommendation. One worksheet per risk;
> the results feed the Risk Register.

> **How to use this template:** complete one worksheet per risk
> assessed. Reference the RAM for scoring definitions. Scores are 1-5
> per the methodology; the verdict follows the evaluation criteria.
