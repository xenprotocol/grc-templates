> **Purpose:** Structured communication to a specific audience about a
> risk, a risk decision, or a risk posture change. Different audiences
> need different language — this template forces the right framing.

> **How to use this template:** select the audience, adapt the tone
> (staff = practical and personal; board = strategic and decision-
> oriented; regulator = precise and evidence-based), and complete the
> relevant sections. Never include exploit details in staff or
> public-facing communications.
