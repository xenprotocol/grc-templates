> **Purpose:** The board-level view of the risk posture: what changed,
> what is critical, what is being done, and what decisions are needed.
> Executives do not read the risk register — they read this. Keep it to
> one or two pages.

> **How to use this template:** complete quarterly. The top risks table
> shows the highest-scored risks with trend arrows. The decisions section
> is where you ask the board for what you need.
